## Third-party

`Model Assets` contains reference CAD of hardware designed by others, included
so that parts can be designed and rendered against that hardware. It is **not**
covered by this repository's licence and is **not** armpitMFG's to license.
All rights remain with their respective owners:

  - `Model Assets/Misc` - INIU P63-E1 power bank. Model contributed by
    **Sherman_Strike**; the product is INIU's.
  - `Model Assets/Motors` - 42AIM15, 42AIM30, 57AIM30, iHSV57 servo motors.
    Property of the respective motor manufacturers.
  - `Model Assets/Reference Board` - OSSM Reference Board v2.3 and cutout
    templates. Property of the OSSM project.
  - `Model Assets/Remote Board` - OSSM Remote PCB. Property of the OSSM
    project.

`OSSM Tools/just_b Wrench` is a third-party design by **just_b**, included with
credit and not licensed by armpitMFG.
