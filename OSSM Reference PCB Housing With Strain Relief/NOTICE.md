## Modifications by armpitMFG

  - Added a power-plug strain relief with a 12.5mm inner diameter
  - Reduced the size and number of holes on the top for a more minimal look
    and reduced LED brightness
  - Widened the footprint by 2mm total for additional board-fit tolerance
