## Provenance

These parts are drop-in replacements for the OSSM default hinge, originally by
**Shick**. They are dimensionally tied to that design and are treated here as
derived from OSSM project source, so both copyrights are named.

Upstream: <https://github.com/KinkyMakers/OSSM-hardware>

## Modifications by armpitMFG

  - `Hinge 40-4040CL` - experimental hinge at 3.2mm thickness to support 1/8
    inch metal hinges, oriented to print standing at a slight angle for higher
    strength under load
  - `Hinge Plate Knob M8 For Metal` - M8 nut variant using a 0.05mm wall slice
    down the sides of the positioning key, so slicers generate deep internal
    walls for shear strength
  - `Hinge Lever Bushing` - M8 quick-release lever bushing supporting washer
    inserts up to 15mm
